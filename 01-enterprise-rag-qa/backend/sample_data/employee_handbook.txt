SECTION 1: PAID TIME OFF (PTO) POLICY
Acme Corp provides all full-time employees with 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO accrues at a rate of 1.66 days per month. Unused PTO up to 5 days can be carried over into the next calendar year, but must be used by March 31st. Employees must request PTO at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal.

SECTION 2: HYBRID & REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees in eligible roles are permitted to work remotely up to 3 days per week (typically Tuesdays and Thursdays are designated as core in-office collaboration days). Employees working remotely are eligible for a one-time $500 home office equipment stipend upon hiring. High-speed internet expenses up to $75 per month are reimbursable upon submitting monthly receipts.

SECTION 3: HEALTH & WELLNESS BENEFITS
Health insurance coverage begins on the first day of employment. Acme Corp covers 90% of individual medical premiums and 75% of family plan premiums. In addition, all employees receive a $100 monthly wellness allowance that can be applied toward gym memberships, mental health apps, or fitness equipment.

SECTION 4: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS & BONUSES
Performance evaluations occur bi-annually in June and December. Annual performance bonuses are calculated based on individual goals achievement and company financial performance, targeting 10% to 15% of annual base salary. Merit salary increases are announced in January following the December review cycle.

SECTION 5: PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
Acme Corp supports continuous learning and offers up to $2,500 annually per employee for tuition reimbursement, industry certifications, conferences, and technical training courses. Requests require pre-approval from your direct manager.
